# How to Add Assets to Glossary Terms

After creating a glossary term, data assets can be associated with the term. In the **Glossary Term > Assets Tab** all the assets associated with the glossary term are displayed. These data assets are further subgrouped as Tables, Topics, Dashboards, etc.

<img src="/public/images/how-to-guides/governance/term3.png" alt="Assets Tab" />

You can add more assets by clicking on **Add > Assets**.

<img src="/public/images/how-to-guides/governance/asset.png" alt="Add Asset" />

You can further search and filter assets by type. Simply select the relevant assets and click **Save**.

<img src="/public/images/how-to-guides/governance/asset1.png" alt="Assets Related to the Glossary Term" />

OpenMetadata saves each asset as its own change, with its own version, updated-by user, and change event. OpenMetadata also checks your permission on each asset. An asset you cannot edit is reported with the reason, and the other assets are still added. An asset held by an approval workflow is reported as **Pending approval**. A selected column is saved through its table. An asset is refused if its tags are mutually exclusive with the tags the term adds.

The glossary term lists the Assets, which makes it easy to discover all the data assets related to the term.

By default, a table can be associated with more than one glossary term. Admins can restrict this to a single term per table in [Data Asset Rules](/v2.2.x-SNAPSHOT/how-to-guides/admin-guide/data-asset-rules) using the **Tables can only have a single Glossary Term** rule.

## Only Approved Terms Can Be Applied

Only **Approved** glossary terms show on data assets. The same rule applies in the UI and through the API.

- **UI**: The term picker offers only Approved terms. **Add** > **Assets** is disabled on a term that is not Approved.
- **API**: A request that applies a **Draft**, **In Review**, **Rejected**, or **Deprecated** term to an asset or to one of its columns or fields succeeds, but OpenMetadata holds the term as pending. A pending term is stored but not shown anywhere, including the UI, the API, search, and exports, until the term is **Approved**. It then appears on the asset with no new write. This covers create, `PUT`, `PATCH`, and bulk requests, a term's `assets/add` request, a table's data model, and the column bulk-update and CSV previews.
- **Bots and automation**: Bots follow the same rule. A write that applies a term that is not Approved succeeds for that entity, and the term stays pending until it is approved.
- **Terms already applied**: A term that is already applied to an asset or column stays on it, even if the term later leaves the Approved status. A write that carries the same term back to the same asset or column still succeeds.
- **Terms without a status**: A term created before the approval workflow existed has no status and counts as Approved.
- **Classification tags**: The rule applies to glossary terms only.

## Glossary Terms and Tags

If **Tags** are associated with a **Glossary Term**, then applying that glossary term to a data asset, will also automatically apply the associated tags to that data asset. For example, the glossary term ‘Account’ has a PII.Sensitive tag associated with it. When you add a glossary term to a data asset, the associated tags also get added.
