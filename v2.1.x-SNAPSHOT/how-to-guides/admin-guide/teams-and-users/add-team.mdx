# How to Add a Team

Creating a Team in OpenMetadata is easy. Decide on the `teamType` that you would like to add. Refer to the [**Team Structure in OpenMetadata**](/v2.1.x-SNAPSHOT/how-to-guides/admin-guide/teams-and-users) to get a clear understanding of the various **Team Types**.

**1.** Click on **Settings** > **Members** > **Teams**. Further, navigate to the relevant `BusinessUnit`, `Division`, or `Department` where you would like to create a new team. Click on **Add Team**.

<img src="/public/images/how-to-guides/teams-and-users/members-teams-card.png" alt="Members page with the Teams card highlighted" />

<img src="/public/images/how-to-guides/teams-and-users/members-organization-teams.png" alt="Organization team page with the Add Team button" />

**2.** Enter the details like `Name`, `Display Name`, `Email`, `Team Type`, and `Description` and click on **Save**. The choice of the `teamType` is restricted by the type of the parent team selected. More information can be found in the [**Team Structure**](/v2.1.x-SNAPSHOT/how-to-guides/admin-guide/teams-and-users) document. Enable the **Public Team** option to allow open access, permitting anyone to join the team, view data, and collaborate.

<Warning>
- Once created, the teamType for `Group` **cannot be changed later**.
- Only the Teams of the type `Group` can **own data assets**.
</Warning>

<img src="/public/images/how-to-guides/teams-and-users/add-team-form.png" alt="Add Team form with Name, Display Name, Email, Team Type, Public Team, and Domains" />

**3.** The new team has been created. You can further add Users or create another Team within it.

<img src="/public/images/how-to-guides/teams-and-users/add-team-created.png" alt="Organization team page listing the new team" />

## Team Actions

After you create a team, open it to use the team menu (the **...** button in the team header). The menu offers these actions:

- **Export Team**: Export the team. Available for teams that are not of type `Group`. It requires edit access on the team, except for the `Organization` team.
- **Import Team**: Import team data. It has the same availability as **Export Team** and also requires permission to create teams.
- **Make private** or **Make public**: Switch whether anyone can join the team. Not available for the `Organization` team.
- **Restore Team**: Restore a deleted team.
- **Delete**: Delete the team. Requires the **Delete** permission and is not available for the `Organization` team.

OpenMetadata hides the menu button when none of these actions are available to you.
